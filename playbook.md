# Compass Recruiting Playbook (v1 — living document)

These are starting points, not the answer. The whole point of the Call Lab is to find out which
of these lines actually work for *you*, in *your* markets. Version every change (A1 → A2) so the
data stays comparable.

> Verify every specific claim (splits, fees, Concierge terms, office resources) with your managing
> broker before using it on a call. Bracketed text = fill in for your market.

---

## 1. Openers (first 10 seconds — test these hardest)

**Opener A — Permission-based**
> "Hi [Name], this is [You] with Compass. I know I'm calling out of the blue — do you have 30
> seconds for me to tell you why I called, and you can tell me if it's worth a longer chat?"

**Opener B — Market observation**
> "Hi [Name], [You] with Compass here. I was looking at your listing on [street/area] — nice work.
> Quick question: how's the [city] market treating you this year compared to last?"

**Opener C — Direct value**
> "Hi [Name], [You] with Compass. I'll be quick — I talk with productive agents in [city] about
> what they'd need to see to grow their business, and your name came up. Is now a bad time?"

## 2. Core script (after the opener lands)

1. **Discover (ask, don't pitch)**
   - "What's working best for you in your business right now?"
   - "If you could change one thing about your current brokerage, what would it be?"
   - "Where do you want your business to be in two years?"
2. **Bridge** — repeat back their answer in one sentence.
3. **Relevant value (pick ONE that matches their answer)**
   - Marketing / brand → Compass marketing tools and design support
   - Tech / time → the Compass platform (CRM, marketing center, collections)
   - Listings / sellers → Compass Concierge, Private Exclusives
   - Growth / team → [team support / office resources in your market]
4. **Ask for the meeting (small, specific)**
   - "Would it be crazy to grab 20 minutes [day] or [day] so I can show you how agents here use it?
     No pressure to move — worst case you leave with a couple of ideas."

## 3. Voicemail (keep under 20 seconds)

> "Hi [Name], [You] with Compass. I had a quick question about your business in [city] — not
> urgent. [Number], again [Number]. Thanks [Name]."

## 4. Objection bank

| Objection | Response to test |
|---|---|
| "I'm happy where I am." | "That's great — honestly most of the agents I talk to are. I'm not asking you to move today; I'm asking for 20 minutes so if anything ever changes, you know what's out there." |
| "What's the split?" | "Depends on your production and what you need — I'd rather show you real numbers than throw one out. What does your current setup look like?" |
| "Switching is too much hassle." | "Fair — what would the hassle be for you specifically? [listen] Here's how transitions usually work…" |
| "Just send me an email." | "Happy to. So I send something useful — is it marketing, tech, or splits you'd care most about?" |
| "I'm busy." | "Totally — when's a better 2 minutes, later today or tomorrow morning?" |
| "Recruiters call me all the time." | "I bet — you must be doing something right. I'll make it worth it or I won't call again. Fair?" |
| "Compass is too corporate / I heard about layoffs." | "Appreciate you being straight. What specifically did you hear? [listen, answer facts only]" |
| "I'm on a team." | "Is the decision yours or your team lead's? Would it make sense to include them?" |
| "I'm under contract / non-compete." | "Understood — when does that open up? I'll put a note to reconnect a month before." |

## 5. Testing method — finding the formula

- **One variable at a time.** Change the opener *or* the market *or* the time block — not all three.
- **Name every version.** "Opener B2 — added listing reference." Log the version on every call.
- **Sample before verdict.** ~30 connects per variant minimum; ignore anything under 10 dials.
- **Separate the funnel.** Low connect rate → list quality / time of day. Low positive rate →
  opener + discovery. Low meeting rate → the close/ask.
- **Write it down.** Hypothesis before, result after, in the Experiments tab. Patterns you don't
  write down disappear.

## 6. Variables worth testing

- Time block (8–10am, 12–1pm, 4–6pm) and weekday
- State / city / brokerage they're at now
- Agent tenure (new <2y, 2–10y, veteran 10y+) and production tier
- Opener A vs B vs C; a personal listing reference vs none
- Ask size: 20-min coffee vs 10-min call vs office tour
